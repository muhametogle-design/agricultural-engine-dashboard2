GIS POLYGON-TO-PLANT SUITABILITY UPDATE
Build: d4fa740

1. Extract this ZIP completely.
2. Double-click RUN-ABAAR.bat.
3. A fresh server starts on port 8765 and opens the main GIS automatically.

HOW TO USE:
- Draw a new Beer polygon or click an existing polygon.
- The Plant Selector / LIMS Master Database opens automatically.
- Choose Trees, Midho, Khudaar or Saliidda Abuurka and click a plant.
- The selected Beer receives a Green, Yellow or Red suitability card.

THE CARD CHECKS:
- Ciid / Dhoobo soil classification
- measured/saved pH and soil EC
- aquifer depth at the polygon center
- nearest groundwater conductivity and salinity
- drainage/root profile and arid/riverine climate water demand
- bilingual reasons and recommended amendments/irrigation actions

The package also includes Dawaad rainfall/VCI/water-point monitoring and pastoral aquifer, solar-pump and fence tools. Suitability output is decision support and must be verified with field/laboratory tests, VES and water-quality analysis.
